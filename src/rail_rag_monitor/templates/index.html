Rail RAG monitor

A daily synthetic probe of the rail RAG demo, a summary of its real traffic, and the alert rules that watch both.

Status

    The quality alert fires when the last __RECENT__ probe runs fail more often than the up to __BASELINE_MAX__ runs before them, with a one-sided Fisher exact test at p < 0.01 and a fail-rate rise of at least δ = __DELTA__ (__DELTA_NOTE__).

    Fisher’s exact test (p < 0.01) is the gate that decides; δ is a secondary margin that keeps a small, probably random rise from alerting. Two more rules run alongside: uptime (2 consecutive runs with an HTTP error) and latency (warm p95 above __LATENCY_FACTOR__× the baseline median on 2 consecutive runs). None of them alert until __MIN_BASELINE__ baseline runs exist.
